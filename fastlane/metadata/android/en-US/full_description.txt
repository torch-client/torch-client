Torch is a Minecraft client rebuilt in Rust.
It supports 26.1.1 and 1.21.11 servers.

Requirements:
- 64-bit ARM device
- Android 9 or newer

Network use:
- On first launch Torch downloads the game's assets (textures, models, language files) from Mojang's asset server.
- Optional sign in using Minecraft account connects to Microsoft/Mojang's servers to authenticate
- Player skins and capes are downloaded from Mojang's servers (during gameplay)

Torch is in early development. Some features are missing and things might be broken.
https://github.com/torch-client/torch-client/issues

Torch is not affiliated with Mojang Studios or Microsoft.
